Keepguard - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Keepguard is made by FusilliGames (gamesoffusilli@gmail.com). This policy explains what data the game handles.

What the game stores on your device

Your save game, settings (language, volume, display) and, if you turn on Settings > Show Performance, a local performance log. These stay on your device. On Steam, your save may be copied to Steam Cloud so it follows you between computers; that is handled by Valve under Steam's privacy policy. On Android, if you use Google Play Games, your save may be stored in Google Play Games Saved Games, handled by Google under Google's privacy policy.

Leaderboards

If you use the online leaderboards, your highest wave and your platform display name are sent to the platform's leaderboard service: Steam (Valve) on PC, Google Play Games (Google) on Android. We do not run our own server and do not receive this data except as shown in the leaderboard.

Purchases

In-game purchases are processed by Steam or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details.

What we don't do

No advertising, no analytics or tracking, no selling or sharing of personal data, no account with us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can delete your local save at any time in Settings > Delete Save File. For data held by Steam or Google, use their account and privacy settings. For any question or request, email gamesoffusilli@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
