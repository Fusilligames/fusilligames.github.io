Road Games - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Road Games is a family road-trip game made by FusilliGames. This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: the games themselves collect nothing. The only information that leaves your phone is what Google's ad service needs to show an occasional ad, and buying "Remove ads" switches that off.

What the app stores on your phone

These are saved in a small file on your phone only, so the game remembers them next time:

  • Player names you type in ("Who's in the car?"). First names or nicknames are all that's needed.
  • Game progress, such as a running Arrival Bet (the destination you typed and everyone's guessed times), Alphabet Spotter letters, where you got to in an adventure story, and which story endings you've found.
  • Small records, such as your best Plate Hunt A-to-Z time, how many times the app has been opened (so no ads appear the first time), and whether you've bought "Remove ads".

None of this is ever sent anywhere. All of it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Ads

Every game in Road Games is free. Now and then, at most once every 10 minutes and only between games, the app shows an ad from Google AdMob. Ads are never shown the first time you use the app, and never during a game.

  • Ads are requested as family-friendly and non-personalised: the app tells Google they're for a family audience, and ads are not tailored to you based on your activity.
  • To show and count ads, and to prevent ad fraud, Google AdMob may collect limited device information, such as an app-specific identifier for counting ads, your approximate location (from your IP address), and how ads are viewed. The app does not use your phone's advertising ID. This is handled by Google under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • In the European Economic Area and the UK you'll be asked for consent first, as the law requires.
  • With no internet connection, no ads are shown and the games work as normal.

"Remove ads"

A one-time purchase through Google Play removes all ads for good. Google handles the payment. We never see or store your payment details; we only learn from Google whether "Remove ads" has been bought on your Google account. Once it has, the app stops requesting ads entirely.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or registration.
  • No analytics or tracking of your games, and no crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • We do not sell any information.

Children

Road Games is made for families to play together, including children. The games collect no personal information from anyone. Ads are requested in line with Google Play's Families policy: family-friendly, non-personalised, and from Google's certified ad network only.
